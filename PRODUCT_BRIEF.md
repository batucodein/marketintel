# MarketIntel — Product Brief

## What is MarketIntel?

MarketIntel is a B2B trade intelligence platform that helps exporters find, evaluate, and connect with international buyers. Instead of spending weeks manually researching potential customers in foreign markets, exporters use MarketIntel to discover verified importers, understand their buying patterns, and reach out with personalized outreach — all from one platform.

Think of it as a smart sales assistant for international trade. You tell it what you sell, and it finds the companies most likely to buy from you, scores them by potential, and helps you start conversations.

---

## Who is it for?

- **Exporters** looking to expand into new markets
- **Trade companies** managing multiple product lines across countries
- **Sales teams** in manufacturing and commodity businesses (marble, textiles, food, chemicals, etc.)
- **Trade consultants** helping clients find overseas buyers

---

## Core Features

### 1. Smart Discovery

Tell MarketIntel what product you export (e.g., "marble stone," "olive oil," "steel pipes") and it automatically:

- Identifies the correct international trade classification (HS Code)
- Analyzes global trade flows to find the best target countries
- Ranks countries by opportunity using real import/export data
- Discovers actual importing companies in each market

This replaces weeks of manual research with a guided, step-by-step process that takes minutes.

### 2. Verified Importer Database

MarketIntel doesn't guess who might be a buyer — it works with real customs and shipment data. For each potential buyer, you can see:

- **Import history** — how much they import, how often, from which countries
- **Product match** — whether they already import your exact product category
- **Company profile** — website, phone, email, address, business type
- **Existing suppliers** — who they currently buy from (your competitors)

Data comes from customs records, business directories, and company websites — not assumptions.

### 3. AI-Powered Lead Scoring

Every discovered business gets scored on five dimensions:

- **Import Volume** (30%) — Are they importing enough to justify an international deal?
- **Purchase Activity** (25%) — Are they actively importing, or was it a one-time shipment?
- **Reachability** (25%) — Can you actually contact them? Do they have email, phone, website?
- **Product Fit** (15%) — Does your product match what they buy?
- **Recency** (5%) — How recently did they last import?

Scores are based on real data, not guesses. A company with no contact information or no import history gets a low score — no matter how good the name sounds.

### 4. Contact Intelligence

MarketIntel goes beyond just finding company names. It actively enriches every lead with:

- **Email addresses** — ranked by usefulness (sales@, purchasing@, info@)
- **Email verification** — checks if the email actually works before you use it
- **Phone numbers** — extracted from company websites and directories
- **WhatsApp** — detected automatically (common in international trade)
- **Social media** — LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram profiles
- **Contact forms** — flagged when a website has a contact or inquiry form
- **Products & services** — what the company actually sells or buys, extracted from their website

All of this is gathered automatically so your sales team spends time selling, not researching.

### 5. Email Marketing & Outreach

Once you've identified your best leads, MarketIntel helps you reach out:

- **Personalized emails** — AI generates tailored emails for each business based on their profile, import history, and needs. No generic templates — each email references what they actually buy and why your product is relevant.
- **Smart follow-ups** — Schedule and send follow-up sequences. The system adapts messaging based on whether the lead opened, replied, or didn't respond.
- **Buyer needs analysis** — Understand what each business is looking for based on their import patterns, current suppliers, and product categories. Use this to craft offers that speak directly to their situation.
- **Campaign management** — Organize outreach by market, product, or discovery. Track which emails were sent, opened, and replied to.
- **Response tracking** — See which leads are engaging and which aren't, so your team focuses on warm prospects.

The goal is simple: turn discovered leads into real conversations, and real conversations into deals.

### 6. Market Analysis

For each target market, MarketIntel provides:

- **Market size estimates** based on real trade data
- **Demand and saturation scores** — is this market growing or crowded?
- **Competitive landscape** — which countries are already selling there
- **City-level breakdown** — where the buyers are concentrated
- **AI-generated market summary** — plain-language insights your team can act on

### 7. Cost Tracking

Every AI operation in the platform is tracked and reported:

- Total cost across all discoveries
- Cost per individual discovery
- Cost breakdown by AI model
- Daily cost trends

Full transparency — you always know what you're spending.

## How It Works (User Journey)

1. **Enter your product** — "marble stone," "organic honey," etc.
2. **Review target countries** — MarketIntel ranks the best markets for your product
3. **Explore a country** — Upload customs data or let the system find importers
4. **Review scored leads** — See businesses ranked by buying potential and reachability
5. **Deep-dive into leads** — View full profiles, import history, contact details, strengths and weaknesses
6. **Create outreach campaigns** — Generate personalized emails for your top leads
7. **Send and track** — Deliver emails, schedule follow-ups, monitor engagement
8. **Close deals** — Focus your team's time on the leads that respond
